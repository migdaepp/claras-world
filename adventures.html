🌈 Meet the Cast

This is Skittles, the Rainbow Sloth.

Skittles lives in a sparkly forest where the leaves change color every time you giggle. He moves very slowly, but he has the biggest heart of any sloth in the whole world. He's Teddy's best friend (after Clara, of course!).

Did you know? Skittles wears tiny rainbow glasses because the sunbeams are extra sparkly in his forest. ✨
